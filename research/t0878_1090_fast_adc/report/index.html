Recommendation: maybe. Don't start a rev yet. The model says amplitude sampling is worth about 9.8 dB at the 90 % point. Most of that comes from detecting the preamble softly instead of with the PIO's 40-sample hard match, and about 6.1 dB of it is reachable in firmware on the current 1090U.

Order of work:

  1. Bench test of the PIO mismatch budget at 8 (hours). Expected +1.8 dB at 90 %.
  2. Software soft preamble on a continuous 12 Msps comparator stream, TL lowered to 300 mV (1–2 weeks). Expected +6.1 dB. The estimated cost is ~35–50 % of core 1 at 12k bursts/s.
  3. In parallel, a Pico 2 + 8-bit ADC tap on TP2 (RF_DET_OUT) of a 1090U (~2 weeks, ~$30). It records live sky and Pluto sweeps for an offline demod and measures what the ADC adds over step 2.
  4. Rev only if step 3 shows ≥2–3 dB over step 2, or if the MLAT, RSSI and overlap gains are wanted. The rev would be an RP2350 with an 8-bit parallel ADC (6 MSBs wired is enough) plus a buffer, about $1.5–4.5 BOM.

Confidence. The ranking is medium confidence. The absolute dB figures are low-to-medium confidence: the model is calibrated on one bench point (±2.5 dB), the detector floor is inferred, and there is no interference model. Across the sensitivity runs (detector floor 0 to +6 dB, video 5 MHz), the ADC gain at 90 % stays at 8–11 dB and the firmware soft-preamble gain at 6 dB (sensitivity).

"Soft preamble" means a software detector that compares chip averages: min(pulse chips 0, 2, 7, 9) − mean(quiet chips 4, 5, 11–14) > threshold. The threshold is set for ≤1,000 false triggers/s on noise; a 100/s threshold costs ~0.5 dB. Bits are decided by chip-sum comparison, with the same 1–2 bit CRC fix and 3 offset retries as #398.

Why the PIO matcher is the limit

  • The hard match is strict. The matcher must see ≥36 of 40 samples right. Near the threshold, single samples flip on noise, and one miss in four preambles is enough to lose the frame.
  • Bit decisions are not the limit. Feeding ADC samples into the demod but keeping the PIO trigger gains almost nothing at TL 600. The trigger sets today's limit.
  • A lower TL makes things worse. At TL 300 the matchers are tied up by noise edges (1.4 M/s): weak frames improve, but ~10 % of strong frames are lost. This is why the TL can't simply be lowered today.
  • A soft detector averages the noise out. It averages ~12 independent noise samples per chip (24 MHz RF bandwidth × 0.5 µs) and doesn't care about chatter.

The ADC spans the detector's whole range (noise −15 dB to saturation, ~1.3 V). 6 bits (1.1 dB steps) lose ≤0.4 dB. At 10 Msps over the full range: 3-bit -0.7 dB, 4-bit +5.2 dB, unquantized +9.8 dB. Rate barely matters: 8 → 20 Msps is worth ~0.4 dB. A 3-comparator "flash" with thresholds near the floor (+2, +5, +10 dB) gets +7.2 dB; the thresholds must sit near the noise, not across the full range. An I/Q front end with a ±3 MHz channel filter (no log detector) gets +10.7 dB, about 1 dB more than the log-detector ADC, at the cost of a downconverter and two ADCs.

2. Overlapping frames

Two DF17 frames, both 30 dB above noise; the second starts 8–120 µs into the first, with random carrier phase and frequency (±200 kHz), so the two beat. 240 pairs per point.

  • Today: the first frame survives when it is ≥6 dB stronger. The second frame is never captured: the 118 µs guard drops it, as t-0870 measured on air.
  • ADC + soft detector: the first frame survives at ≥4 dB, and a later frame that is ≥4 dB stronger is found inside the first and decoded. Within a decoded frame, only a preamble ≥3 dB stronger may retrigger (readsb style). This cuts self-triggers from ~31 to ~5 per frame; mean 15 soft triggers per 260 µs window.
  • Expected value: per overlap, today recovers 0.39 frames and the ADC 0.89, weighted by the live-sky level-difference distribution (t-0870 RSSI of 97k captures; 59 % of random pairs differ by ≥4 dB). At t-0870's 3,257 expected two-aircraft overlaps/h, the ADC adds about 1,629 frames/h (0.49 %). The hybrid (PIO trigger + ADC demod) adds 467/h. This is small next to the sensitivity gain and grows in proportion to traffic.

  • Today's timing is a port of DataStart48MHzCounts (mid-bit HI counts over 24 bits). It gives 5–13 ns RMS in the model; release notes for #308 measured 15 ns RMS.
  • ADC timing uses mid-level crossings of the ADC samples at chip edges, averaging rise and fall. It gives 1–8 ns, and the rest is mostly transponder rise/fall spread (random 50–100 / 50–200 ns per frame).
  • Level-dependent shift over 25 dB: today 42 ns, ADC 9–10 ns. Receivers that see an aircraft at different levels disagree less, which helps MLAT more than the RMS figure does.
  • RSSI comes from the median ADC level of the frame's pulse chips (0.1 dB σ). Today's method samples the 10 µs RSSI low pass 43 µs into the frame and scales it by 53 %, giving 0.4–1.6 dB σ, worse at high level. An ADC also gives RSSI per frame when frames overlap and frees the shared RP2040 ADC.

Pins. An 8-bit ADC needs 8 data pins and a clock; PIO in pins needs the data pins consecutive. Since 6 bits lose ≤0.4 dB, wiring only D7..D2 is enough. On the current pin map, dropping the comparator path frees GPIO 19–24 (PULSES, 4 DEMOD, RECOVERED_CLK: 6 consecutive) plus 26 and 27 (TL PWM, TL ADC). That fits 6 data bits on 19–24 with the clock on 26.

So a 30-GPIO part works for the full-software design. A hybrid that keeps the comparator and PIO matchers needs the RP2350B.

The half-chip scan shows no measurable loss in the model (8 Msps, step 2 samples vs every sample). Core 1 is 0–2 % busy today (t-0870). DMA load is 8–20 MB/s, trivial. A 4 KB ring holds 0.5 ms at 8 Msps.

Analog front end changes

  • Buffer the AD8313 output. It sources ~400 µA through the 1 k pullup, and the schematic warns to keep VOUT capacitance low. A rail-to-rail op-amp with ≥50 MHz GBW, followed by a 2-pole anti-alias filter at ~0.4 fs, costs ~$0.3.
  • Set the ADC reference to the detector span. Use about 0.6–1.9 V: noise floor ~0.85 V, saturation ~1.77 V (r1090_rssi.hh).
  • More RF gain before the detector: not worth it on its own. BLB01 S21 at 1.1 GHz is 18–20 dB, so the in-band noise reaches the AD8313 at about −67 dBm, near its −64 dBm lower range limit. In the model, +10 dB of gain helps today's chain by only ~1.3 dB (TL retuned to 900) and doesn't help the ADC, because a soft detector isn't hurt by a steady detector floor. It also saturates 10 dB earlier.
  • Narrower SAW. t-0061 estimated ~1.4 dB for TA3003 as X2, but the part is unavailable. It is less important for a soft detector, which averages the wide band.

Validation against real units

  • TL offset. The t-0870 unit ran TL 600 (its log). In the model, a detector floor of −20 dB makes the comparator chatter on noise (1.7 M edges/s at TL 600), which ties up the matchers and loses 15–20 % of strong frames. Real units don't do that. A floor of +3 dB gives a 100 % plateau at TL 600. This matches the gain chain: 2 × BLB01 (S21 ≈ 18–20 dB at 1.1 GHz) minus 2 × 1.9 dB SAW loss puts the noise at ≈ −67 dBm at the AD8313, against its ≈ −64 dBm lower range limit.
  • Sample errors. In real 1-bit captures (40,000 clean frames from t-0870), the off-chip HI rate is a median 0.2–0.7 % even in weak frames. The model gives 0–1.6 %.
  • Timing. The model's comparator timing jitter is 5–13 ns RMS, against 15 ns measured for #308.
  • Implied NF. NF = 4.1 dB. The gain chain suggests ~1.5–2 dB. Today's chain may therefore be ~2 dB worse than modeled, or the bench offset may be within its ±2.5 dB.

Not modeled: Mode A/C fruit, DME and other interference (they raise trigger rates and CPU load more than they change sensitivity); transponder frequency error beyond ±200 kHz; temperature drift of the detector slope and intercept; ADC aperture jitter.

7. Prototype plan

  1. Firmware, today's hardware.
    • Set mismatch_budget to 8 in capture.pio and sweep on the pi-1 Pluto bench against main.
    • Then build a soft-preamble build: one PIO SM streams the comparator continuously at 12 Msps into a DMA ring, and core 1 runs a bit-sliced preamble prefilter, the chip-sum score and the existing demod. Sweep TL 300–600.
  2. ADC tap (~$30, no new PCB).
    • Use a Pico 2 (RP2350) and an AD9280/ADC08100 breakout behind an op-amp buffer on TP2 (RF_DET_OUT) of a 1090U, capturing 8-bit at 10–20 Msps to a ring. Dump raw streams over USB.
    • Run the offline demod in t0878/sim/model.py (soft_search, adc_demod) on Pluto level sweeps and live sky, next to the 1090U's own decodes of the same signal.
  3. 2-bit flash, if the tap shows that amplitude helps. Three of the 12 MCP6566 bodge boards on panel_proto_data_slicer_board on the same detector node, with thresholds from PWMs at noise floor +2/+5/+10 dB, sampled by PIO as 3 bits at 12 Msps. Model: +7.2 dB, for about $1 of parts and 3 GPIOs.
  4. Rev, only after 1–3. RP2350A (6 data bits on the freed GPIO 19–24) or RP2350B (keep the comparator for a hybrid), 8-bit ADC, buffer.
