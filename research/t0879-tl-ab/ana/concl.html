  • Best offset. There is no clear winner. The tradeoff is the same on both sides of 600.
    • Lower TL reaches farther: at 450, range p99 is +15% and fixes at 50–75 nm are 2×. But it decodes fewer frames from near, strong aircraft. Position rate at 15–30 nm: −22% at 400, −11% at 450, −8% at 500. Frames per aircraft at 450: −25%.
    • 400 also brings ~3× more single-frame ICAOs (likely false decodes) and +21% CRC fails.
    • 800 drops half the aircraft beyond 75 nm, with no significant gain up close (+1% to +8%).
    • Overall, total good frames/s and unique aircraft do not differ from 600 by more than the noise for any arm.
  • Default 600 → 450? Not on this evidence. On pi-0, 450 trades ~10% of strong-aircraft updates for more far range, and total frames do not rise. The bench gain (+1.4 dB on pi-1) is real on a quiet floor. pi-0's floor is ~14× noisier, so the best offset depends on the site or unit, not one number.
  • Does the learner find it? It learned 678 mV here, versus 353 on the pi-1 bench. It moves the right way for a noisy floor: noise duty fell from 12.5% to 9.7%. Its single night block was within noise of 600 on every metric. It does not pick 450–500 here, and one block can't show whether 678 beats 600.
  • Matcher-ring mechanism? Partly confirmed, much weaker than t-0877 modeled.
    • Lowering TL raises between-frame noise edges steadily, from 0.8 M/s at 600 to 1.3 M/s at 400.
    • Near-aircraft decodes fall in step, which is the signature of matchers busy with noise.
    • But at 600 the loss is small: going to 800 recovers only +1% to +8% (not significant).

t-0877, revisited

  • The 14% noise duty was pi-0, not the 1090U. pi-0 runs 12.5% HI between frames at TL 600 (813k edges/s), against 0.87% (63k/s) on pi-1. On-signal duty is only slightly higher (13.9%), so the floor itself is high.
  • The Pluto is not the cause. Powering its TX down on pi-0 did not change the duty. t-0875 found no idle LO leak on pi-1. The CW-leak explanation for the bench gap is not supported. pi-0's floor has another, unknown cause: local RF or the unit. A 1-minute 50 Ω termination on pi-0's input would separate the two; that needs a person at the unit.
  • "Raise the default to 800" was wrong. On air, 800 loses distant aircraft and gains little up close. The model's matcher loss at 600 was overstated, even on this noisy unit.
  • Still holds: the direction of the tradeoff. Noise edges cost strong frames; a lower TL helps weak ones.

Caveats:

  • Rounds 4–5 ran in night traffic (rounds 1–3 in busy traffic). Traffic fell ~40× overnight, so effects come from a model that removes the time trend.
  • The ~100 comparisons here include some false positives. Example: 450's −23% total frames is not monotone with 400 and 500.
  • CPR jumps at 800 (×6.8) come from three day blocks with single-digit counts.
  • Range is measured from an estimated center.
  • Demod CPU is ≤1.4% of core 1 at every arm. Time per demod is 3% lower at ≤500 and 6% higher at 800, so it is negligible.
