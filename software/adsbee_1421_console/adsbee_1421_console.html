Firmware Update

Flashing erases the current app image. The device will not run until a flash completes successfully (the ROM bootloader stays available for retries — the board cannot be bricked by this process).

ADSBee 1421 Programmer, or an adapter wired RTS → SYNC and DTR → RESET_N: press Enter bootloader. The page holds SYNC high, pulses reset, and checks that the ROM bootloader answers. After flashing it drives SYNC low again and restarts the module.

Any other adapter: put the module into the bootloader by hand.

  1. Wire the adapter to the module: TX → SURX (pin 20, DIO_2), RX → SUTX (pin 21, DIO_3), and ground to ground.
  2. Hold SYNC (pin 28, DIO_5) high at 3.3 V.
  3. With SYNC still high, reset the module: pull RESET_N (pin 17) low briefly, or power-cycle it.
  4. Release reset and keep SYNC high. On an adapter that wires DTR to RESET_N, Enter bootloader resets the module again, and SYNC must still be high then for it to land back in the bootloader.
  5. Press Enter bootloader below.

The baud rate does not need to match: the ROM locks onto whatever rate this page sends its sync bytes at. If the module boots its application with SYNC held high, its bootloader backdoor may be turned off: AT+BOOTLOADER_PIN? answers 0, and AT+BOOTLOADER_PIN=1,DEADBEE turns it back on.
